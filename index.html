Our Story

A legacy of flavors, crafted with love since generations

What is mogha?

mogha means "flawless" or "perfect" in Sanskrit, and that's our promise to you. We bring the authentic tastes of India to your table with recipes passed down through generations.

From aromatic biryanis to tandoori specialties, every dish is crafted with traditional techniques and the finest ingredients. Our chefs have over 20 years of experience in authentic Indian cuisine.

Our Mission

To create an unforgettable dining experience that celebrates India's rich culinary heritage while bringing people together through exceptional food and warm hospitality.

We use traditional cooking methods including clay oven (tandoor) roasting and slow-cooked curries to ensure authentic flavors in every bite.

Meet Our Chefs

Master Chef Polina

Head Chef & Founder

With over 20 years of culinary expertise in authentic Indian cuisine, Chef Polina brings the rich flavors of Hyderabad and Andhra Pradesh to every dish. Trained in traditional cooking methods passed down through generations, he believes in using only the freshest ingredients and time-honored techniques.

"Every dish I create is a tribute to the incredible culinary heritage of India. At Amogha, we don't just serve food — we serve memories."

Biryani Expert Tandoor Master Andhra Cuisine

Chef Usha

Senior Chef & Culinary Artist

Chef Usha brings a unique blend of traditional South Indian flavors and modern culinary artistry to Amogha. With her deep knowledge of spices and passion for authentic home-style cooking, she crafts dishes that taste just like a warm meal from an Indian kitchen.

"Cooking is love made visible. Every spice I use tells a story of our rich Indian heritage."

South Indian Specialist Spice Expert Home-Style Cuisine

Chef Bhavana

Assistant Chef to Polina

Chef Bhavana works alongside Master Chef Polina, bringing dedication and a keen eye for detail to every dish. With her passion for authentic flavors and commitment to perfection, she ensures that each plate leaving the kitchen meets the highest standards of taste and presentation.

"Great food is made with patience, love, and the right balance of spices."

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer delivery? +

Yes! We offer free delivery for orders over ₹500 within a 5 km radius. Delivery typically takes 30-45 minutes.

Do you have vegetarian options? +

Absolutely! We have an extensive vegetarian menu with over 30 dishes including vegan options.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions? +

Yes, we can adjust spice levels and accommodate most dietary needs. Please inform us when ordering.

Do you offer catering services? +

Yes! We cater for events of all sizes. Contact us at catering@amogha.com for custom menus.

Is parking available? +

Yes, we have free parking available for all guests. The restaurant is also wheelchair accessible.

Do you take reservations? +

Yes! You can book online through our website or call us directly at +91 91210 04999.
